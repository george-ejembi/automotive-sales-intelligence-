# Automotive Sales Intelligence — Analytical Framework

## 1. Framework Overview

The **Automotive Sales Intelligence** project uses a structured analytical framework to transform transactional automotive sales data into decision-ready business intelligence.

The framework progresses from:

```text
Data
  ↓
Metrics
  ↓
Dimensions
  ↓
Business Questions
  ↓
Analysis
  ↓
Insights
  ↓
Decision Support
```

The analysis is primarily **descriptive and diagnostic**.

---

# 2. Analytical Dimensions

The project evaluates performance across five major dimensions.

## 2.1 Time

Time analysis evaluates how sales performance changes across the historical period.

Key metrics:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Units Sold
* Average Order Value
* Sales Growth
* Order Volume

Primary dimensions:

* Year
* Month
* Quarter
* Order Date

### Analytical question

> How is sales activity changing over time?

# 7. KPI Framework

The KPI framework is organized into four analytical groups.

## Sales KPIs

| KPI                   | Definition                       |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Total Sales           | Sum of transaction sales         |
| Total Orders          | Distinct count of orders         |
| Units Sold            | Total quantity ordered           |
| Average Order Value   | Sales divided by distinct orders |
| Average Selling Price | Sales divided by units sold      |

---

## Product KPIs

| KPI                    | Definition                                   |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Product Sales          | Sales generated by a product                 |
| Product Units          | Units sold by product                        |
| Product Contribution % | Product sales as a proportion of total sales |
| Product Orders         | Distinct orders containing the product       |

---

## Customer KPIs

| KPI             | Definition                                 |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Customer Sales  | Revenue attributed to a customer           |
| Customer Orders | Distinct orders associated with a customer |
| Customer Units  | Units purchased by a customer              |
| Customer AOV    | Customer sales divided by customer orders  |

---

## Market KPIs

| KPI                    | Definition                                   |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Country Sales          | Sales generated in a country                 |
| Country Orders         | Orders generated in a country                |
| Country Units          | Units sold in a country                      |
| Country Contribution % | Country sales as a proportion of total sales |
| Country AOV            | Country sales divided by country orders      |

# 12. DAX Measurement Layer

The analytical framework relies on reusable DAX measures.

### Total Sales

```DAX
Total Sales =
SUM(Sales[SALES])
```

### Total Orders

```DAX
Total Orders =
DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[ORDERNUMBER])
```

### Total Units

```DAX
Total Units =
SUM(Sales[QUANTITYORDERED])
```

### Average Order Value

```DAX
Average Order Value =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Sales],
    [Total Orders]
)
```

### Average Selling Price

```DAX
Average Selling Price =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Sales],
    [Total Units]
)
```

### Sales Contribution

```DAX
Sales Contribution % =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Sales],
    CALCULATE(
        [Total Sales],
        ALL(Sales)
    )
)
```

The measurement layer ensures that KPIs remain dynamic when users apply filters.

# 14. Decision-Support Logic

The analytical framework follows a four-stage intelligence model.

```text
LEVEL 1 — DESCRIPTIVE
What happened?

        ↓

LEVEL 2 — DIAGNOSTIC
Where did it happen and which dimensions contributed?

        ↓

LEVEL 3 — COMMERCIAL INTERPRETATION
What patterns or concentrations require attention?

        ↓

LEVEL 4 — DECISION SUPPORT
What business areas should management investigate further?
```

The current project primarily implements Levels 1–3 and provides the analytical foundation for Level 4.

---

# 15. Analytical Limitations

The framework is based on historical transactional data and therefore has several limitations.

### No Causal Inference

Observed relationships do not necessarily establish causation.

### No Profitability Model

Sales revenue should not be interpreted as profit because product cost and operating expenses are not included.

### Historical Context

Historical performance does not automatically represent current market conditions.

### Limited Customer Context

The dataset does not provide comprehensive customer demographic, behavioral, or firmographic information.

### Limited Market Context

External variables such as market size, competitor activity, macroeconomic conditions, and exchange rates are not incorporated.

# 18. Framework Outcome

The analytical framework establishes a repeatable method for transforming automotive transaction data into structured commercial intelligence.

The central principle is:

> **Do not simply report the number. Provide the analytical structure required to understand the number.**

This allows the Automotive Sales Intelligence project to function as a business intelligence system rather than a collection of disconnected visualizations.
