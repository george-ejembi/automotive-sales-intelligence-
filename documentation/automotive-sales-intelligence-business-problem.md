# Automotive Sales Intelligence — Business Problem

## 1. Business Context

The automotive business has accumulated historical transactional data covering orders, products, customers, pricing, sales, geographic markets, and deal sizes.

While this data provides a detailed record of commercial activity, raw transaction-level data does not independently provide management with a clear understanding of overall sales performance, product contribution, customer value, or market performance.

The business therefore requires an analytical framework that transforms transactional data into structured commercial intelligence.

The **Automotive Sales Intelligence** project addresses this requirement by developing an interactive business intelligence solution that enables stakeholders to monitor sales performance, evaluate product and market contribution, understand customer purchasing behavior, and identify areas requiring further commercial investigation.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Management lacks a centralized analytical environment for systematically evaluating automotive sales performance across time, products, customers, geographic markets, and deal sizes.

Without an integrated analytical framework, it is difficult to:

* Determine how sales performance changes over time.
* Identify products and product lines generating the greatest sales contribution.
* Compare commercial performance across geographic markets.
* Identify high-value customers.
* Understand the distribution of transactions across deal-size categories.
* Evaluate order and purchasing activity.
* Detect areas of underperformance requiring further investigation.
* Translate historical sales records into decision-ready business intelligence.

The project therefore seeks to convert historical automotive sales transactions into an interactive analytical system that provides a consistent view of commercial performance.

---

## 3. Business Objective

The primary objective is to develop an **Automotive Sales Intelligence system** capable of answering:

> **What is happening in the automotive sales business, where is it happening, which products, customers, and markets are contributing, and where should management investigate further?**

The project focuses on descriptive and diagnostic analytics rather than automated decision-making.

---

## 4. Analytical Objectives

### 4.1 Sales Performance

Evaluate historical sales performance across time and identify changes in revenue and order activity.

### 4.2 Product Performance

Determine which product lines and individual products contribute most significantly to sales.

### 4.3 Customer Performance

Analyze customer purchasing activity and identify customers with substantial commercial contribution.

### 4.4 Geographic Performance

Compare sales performance across countries and identify differences in market contribution.

### 4.5 Deal-Size Analysis

Evaluate how different deal-size categories contribute to overall sales and transaction volume.

### 4.6 Commercial Diagnostics

Identify patterns, concentrations, and variations in sales performance that warrant further investigation.

---

## 5. Key Business Questions

### Section 1 — Sales & Market Performance

1. How is total sales performance changing over time?
2. Which product lines generate the highest sales?
3. Which countries contribute the most revenue?
4. How does sales performance vary across deal sizes?
5. Which products generate the highest sales value?

### Section 2 — Customer & Commercial Performance

6. Which customers generate the highest revenue?
7. Which customers place the highest number of orders?
8. How does average order value vary across markets?
9. Which product lines demonstrate the strongest commercial contribution?
10. Where are the largest areas requiring further commercial investigation?

## 7. Scope

### Included

The project covers:

* Historical sales transactions.
* Order activity.
* Product performance.
* Customer performance.
* Geographic performance.
* Deal-size analysis.
* Sales trends.
* KPI development.
* Interactive Power BI reporting.

### Excluded

The current analytical scope does not attempt to:

* Forecast future sales.
* Optimize inventory automatically.
* Predict customer churn.
* Determine optimal pricing.
* Perform causal inference.
* Make automated strategic decisions.

These capabilities could be developed in future iterations using additional data and analytical methods.

---

Expected value includes:

Provides a consolidated view of sales performance across major commercial dimensions.

### Faster Analysis

### Product Intelligence

---

## 10. Analytical Philosophy

The project follows a decision-oriented analytical progression:

```text
Raw Transactions
       ↓
Data Quality
       ↓
Structured Data
       ↓
KPIs
       ↓
Business Questions
       ↓
Descriptive Analysis
       ↓
Diagnostic Analysis
       ↓
Commercial Intelligence
```

The purpose is not simply to visualize historical data, but to create a structured environment in which stakeholders can investigate **what happened, where it happened, and which commercial dimensions contributed to the observed performance**.
